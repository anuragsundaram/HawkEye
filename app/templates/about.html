    How to use filters:
    Some views accept SQL-like filter expressions (not raw SQL).
    The internal parser converts them to WHERE statements.
    Supported syntax: [column] [operator] [value] [and | or] [column] ...
    
    Filter examples:
    - sid = 100
    - executions > 1000 and elapsed_time < 5
    - status = 'ACTIVE' or pct_used > 80
    
    Supported operators: =, !=, <, >, <=, >=, LIKE, IS NULL, IS NOT NULL
